#ECOMMERCE PRODUCT COST, DISCOUNT AND PROFIT DASHBOARD

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
This project focuses on organizing imported product data and building a dashboard that helps store owner understand product costs, compare profitability, and make better pricing decisions. Also, know best selling product.

OBJECTIVE: 
1. Product sales amount information and discount prices for goods extraction 
2. Income and Discount Daily, Weekly and Monthly Report generation
3. Payment Account Reconciliation
2. Total revenue and Total profit generated display
3. Provide insight on the actual profit made after discount has been deducted every month

KEY ACTIVITIES: 
1. Extracted the needed columns of data needed for review and analysis
2. Organize and Perform simple cleaning on the product sales data 
3. Generated Daily and Monthly reporting
4. Total Revenue checked
5. Calculate the total selling price for each product category and total discount charges given to customers 
6. Calculate the total revenue on each product category
7. Profit or Loss statement
7. Visualization and Insight Dashboard


WHAT I DID:
1. Structured the product records
2. Reviewed the data for inconsistencies
3. Calculated sales amount
4. Prepared summaries to compare sales amount and discount amount
5. Also prepared a product summary with total product category count, and revenue generated by each product category. 
6. Designed a dashboard to highlight the total revenue, total discount, best selling product (by profit margin), highest selling product (by product count) and make important figures easier to review.

PROJECT OUTCOME:
1. The dashboard provides a clearer view of product salws amount and potential margins 
2. Assisted the business to compare items and make more informed pricing decisions.

KEY INSIGHTS:  
1. Found total discount placed on goods is 0.59% of the total Income
2. Electronics product tends to be the highest selling product with total sale of 9,514 made
3. Electronics also generated the highest revenue (been the best seller product)
4. Found sales went up 70% in March, May and August
5. Found sales dropped 23% in September and November

TOOLS: 
Microsoft Excel, formulas, functions, PivotTables, charts, and dashboard design.

Key Features:
1. Product sales summary.
2. Discount calculation.
3. Profit margin comparison.
4. Best selling and Highest selling products.
5. Visual summary of key figures.
